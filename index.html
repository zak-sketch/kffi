Who We Are

A faith‑based nonprofit with three core callings, united by one mission.

Equipping Pastors & Leaders

We train and disciple pastors and church leaders across Africa with sound biblical teaching. Our network includes 16 instructor pastors and 378 volunteer coordinators serving in 20 nations.

Caring for Orphans

We provide holistic care for orphans and vulnerable children through three programs: Rescued Children (full‑time care), Rehabilitation of Street Children, and Vocational Training for youth.

Rooted in Faith. Driven by Transparency.

We are a faith‑based nonprofit that believes transparency honors God and our donors. Every donation is tracked and reported monthly.

Our Training Team

A continent‑wide network of dedicated servants.

KFFI operates through a dedicated, continent‑wide team:

  • 16 Instructor Pastors from different countries in Africa — our core faculty who are sent to equip pastors.
  • 378 Volunteer Coordinators — active pastors serving across Africa who voluntarily mobilize and coordinate trainings.

How Training is Supported

Our instructors travel extensively to reach pastors in remote towns and villages. To make each training possible, we facilitate their transportation and boarding — costs vary depending on location and mode of transport.

Our coordinators and volunteers serve sacrificially without pay, though any donations to encourage their work are deeply appreciated.

To protect our team and ensure accountability, we do not publish individual instructor profiles or costs online. For all details regarding support, sponsorship, or to facilitate a training, please contact us directly through our official channel.

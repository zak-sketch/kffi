Kingdom Faith Foundation International

One Foundation. Three Callings. One Mission for Africa.

For nearly 20 years — since 2006 — KFFI has been discipling leaders, rescuing street children, and caring for orphans from our home in Kenya to 20 nations across the continent.

Who We Are

📖

Equipping Pastors & Leaders

We train and disciple pastors across Africa with sound biblical teaching. Our network includes 16 instructor pastors and 378 volunteer coordinators in 20 nations.

🧸

Caring for Orphans

Holistic care through three programs: Rescued Children (full-time care), Rehabilitation of Street Children, and Vocational Training for youth.

🤝

Rooted in Faith. Driven by Transparency.

We are a faith-based nonprofit that believes transparency honors God and our donors. Every donation is tracked and reported monthly.

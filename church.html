The People Behind the Mission

Kingdom Faith Ministry International is a community of believers, pastors, families, churches, ministry workers, and partners serving together to strengthen the Church and extend the love of Christ across Africa.

A Shared Work

The Ministry Is Bigger Than Any One Person

Every part of this work is strengthened by people serving alongside one another. Pastors encourage pastors. Churches support churches. Families care for families. Ministry workers share their gifts, and partners help create opportunities for others to grow.

Rev. John Amos Butalia and Carol June Butalia helped establish this ministry and continue to serve within it. Their role is not separate from the community, but part of a wider work in which many people contribute, learn, serve, and grow together.

Rev. John Amos Butalia

Equipping People for Ministry

Rev. John Amos Butalia has served in Gospel ministry for more than two decades. His ministry has been shaped by a desire to see pastors and Gospel workers strengthened in their calling and better equipped to serve the people entrusted to them.

Through Kingdom Faith Ministry International, teaching and ministry opportunities are made available to pastors and ministry workers from different communities and nations. The emphasis is on practical biblical teaching, spiritual growth, responsible leadership, and strengthening the local church.

The ministry has included teaching on hearing and following God's leading, the manifestations of the Holy Spirit, biblical understanding of the coming of the Son of Man, and the importance of healthy relationships and families within ministry.

What began as an itinerant work has grown into a wider network of relationships and ministry opportunities. Love of Calvary Family Church in Kitengela provides a local setting from which people can gather, worship, learn, serve, and encourage one another.

“When the shepherd is strengthened, the flock is strengthened.”

— Rev. John Amos Butalia

Carol June Butalia

Serving Families and the Wider Community

Carol June Butalia is one of the founding servants of Kingdom Faith Ministry International. Her ministry has included caring for families, encouraging women, supporting pastors' families, and helping create an environment where children and adults can experience Christian care and belonging.

Her service reflects the ministry's wider commitment to care for people as a whole. Spiritual teaching is accompanied by practical concern for families, children, relationships, and the everyday needs of people within the community.

Through relationships with women, families, children, and ministry workers, Carol contributes to a culture of encouragement and mutual care. The work is not about one individual providing everything; it is about people using their gifts and responsibilities to strengthen one another.

This same spirit extends to Love of Calvary Family Church, where worship, Bible teaching, fellowship, prayer, and service bring people together as one community.

“When we strengthen families and care for one another, we strengthen the community around us.”

— Carol June Butalia

Growing Together

Everyone Has a Place in the Work

The strength of the ministry comes from participation. People are not simply recipients or providers; they are part of a community where gifts, experience, prayer, encouragement, knowledge, and practical help can be shared.

Learn

Pastors, ministry workers, families, and church members have opportunities to learn through teaching, fellowship, mentoring, and shared experience.

Serve

People contribute according to their gifts and opportunities, helping strengthen churches, families, children, and ministry relationships.

Grow

As people grow in faith, knowledge, relationships, and service, that growth becomes a blessing to others and to the churches and communities around them.

A Community of Mutual Strength

We believe ministry is strongest when people walk together. Those who teach also continue to learn. Those who receive encouragement can encourage others. Those who have resources can share, while those with time, experience, prayer, skills, or practical help can contribute in meaningful ways.

In this way, the work of Kingdom Faith Ministry International belongs to the wider community it serves. The goal is not to create dependence on individuals, but to see people and churches strengthened so that they can strengthen others.

“As each part does its work, the whole body grows and builds itself up in love.”

— Ephesians 4:16

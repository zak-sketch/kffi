Rev. John Amos Butalia

Equipping Pastors, Strengthening the African Church

Rev. John Amos Butalia has served in Gospel ministry for over two decades with a singular burden: to strengthen the African church by equipping its pastors. He is the founder of Kingdom Faith Ministry International, a movement that has trained pastors and Gospel ministers in 17 African nations.

Rev. John observed that many pastors preach with passion but lack the tools to disciple effectively, often unable to afford formal Bible training. KFFI was born to fill that gap. His teaching focuses on grounding ministers in their calling: how to hear the voice of God, minister in power through the nine manifestations of the Holy Spirit, understand the coming of the Son of Man, and build strong marriages and families for ministry.

For years, the work was itinerant — traveling by road and air to reach remote villages. Today, the Lord has established Love of Calvary Family Church, Kitengela, as KFFI's home base — a model church and residential training center that hosts pastors from many nations for intensive equipping.

“I believe the greatest need in Africa is not more churches, but well-equipped pastors who can lead with integrity, power, and love. When the shepherd is strengthened, the flock thrives.”

Co-Founder Since 2006

Carol June Butalia

Compassion & Family Ministry Leader

Carol June Butalia is the devoted wife of Rev. John and Co-Founder of Kingdom Faith Ministry International. She is the heart behind KFFI's compassion work among orphans and vulnerable children, extending the love of Calvary to widows and young families in need.

Carol leads ministry to women, pastors' wives, and children, ensuring that as pastors are trained in truth, families are strengthened in love. Her heart for the vulnerable has shaped KFFI's holistic approach — caring for the whole person: body, mind, and spirit.

With gentle strength and unwavering faith, Carol has been a cornerstone of the ministry, providing a nurturing presence that reflects the love of Christ to all who come through the doors of Love of Calvary Family Church. She mentors young mothers, teaches Bible studies for women, and oversees the children's programs with joy and dedication.

“Every child is a gift from God. When we care for the fatherless and strengthen the hands of mothers, we are building the future of the Church in Africa.”

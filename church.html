Rev. John Amos Butalia & Carol June Butalia — Founders of Kingdom Faith Ministry International

Founder

Rev. John Amos Butalia

Founder, Kingdom Faith Ministry International & Love of Calvary Family Church

Rev. John Amos Butalia has served in Gospel ministry for over two decades, with a burden to strengthen the African church by strengthening its pastors. He is the Founder of Kingdom Faith Ministry International, a ministry that has equipped pastors and Gospel ministers in 17 African countries.

Rev. John observed that many pastors preach with passion but without tools, unable to afford Bible school. KFFI was born to fill that gap. His teaching focuses on grounding ministers in their calling: how to hear the voice of God, ministering in power through the nine manifestations of the Holy Spirit, the coming of the Son of Man, and marriage and family for ministers.

For years this work was itinerant. Today, the Lord has established Love of Calvary Family Church, Kitengela as KFFI's home base — a model church and residential training center for pastors from many nations.

Co-Founder

Carol June Butalia

Co-Founder, Compassion & Family Ministry

Carol is the wife of Rev. John and Co-Founder of the ministry. She is the pioneer of KFFI's compassion work among orphans and vulnerable children, extending the love of Calvary to widows and young families in need.

She leads ministry to women, pastors' wives, and children, ensuring that as pastors are trained in truth, families are strengthened in love. Her heart for the vulnerable has shaped KFFI's holistic approach to caring for the whole person — body, mind, and spirit.

Carol's gentle strength and unwavering faith have been a cornerstone of the ministry, providing a nurturing presence that reflects the love of Christ to all who come through the doors of Love of Calvary Family Church.

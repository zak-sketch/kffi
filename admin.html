Kingdom Faith Foundation International

Equipping Pastors · Caring for Orphans · Strengthening Local Churches

One Foundation. Three Callings. One Mission for Africa.

For nearly 20 years since 2006, KFFI has been discipling leaders, rescuing street children, and caring for orphans from our base in Kenya to 20 nations across the continent.

Who We Are

Equipping Pastors & Leaders

We train and disciple pastors and church leaders across Africa with sound biblical teaching. Our network includes 16 instructor pastors and 378 volunteer coordinators serving in 20 nations.

Caring for Orphans

Holistic care for orphans and vulnerable children through three programs: Rescued Children (full-time care), Rehabilitation of Street Children, and Vocational Training for youth.

Rooted in Faith. Driven by Transparency.

We are a faith-based nonprofit that believes transparency honors God and our donors. Every donation is tracked and reported monthly.

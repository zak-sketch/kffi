Pastor Training — Our Core Calling for 20 Years

Reaching and equipping pastors across 17 African nations with the Word and the Spirit.

For 20 years, Rev. John Amos Butalia and Kingdom Faith Ministry International have traveled across 17 African nations with one mission: to reach and equip pastors and Gospel ministers who love Jesus but have never had the chance to be trained.

We found pastors preaching with passion but without tools. They love the Word, but have never attended Bible school due to cost and financial constraints. KFFI was born to fill that gap.

What We Teach

We ground ministers in the Word and in the Power of the Spirit:

  • How to Hear the Voice of God
  • Ministering in Power — The Nine Manifestations of the Holy Spirit
  • Eschatology — The Coming of the Son of Man
  • Marriage & Family for Ministers
  • Other Foundational Topics for Grounding a Minister in His Calling

How We Train

We don't just give certificates. We train through:

  1. Intensive Seminars in villages and towns across Africa
  2. Residential Training at Love of Calvary, Kitengela
  3. Mentorship — Walking with pastors long after training ends

Our Footprint: 17 Nations

Over two decades, our training has reached pastors in East, Central, and Southern Africa. Each pastor trained means a church strengthened and a village reached.

Love of Calvary Family Church — Our Training Center in Kitengela

After 20 years of traveling across Africa, God gave us a base.

Why Kitengela?

Kitengela is one of the fastest-growing towns in Kenya. Families from Maasailand and Nairobi meet here. The harvest is great, but the labourers are few. God planted us here to be a light.

Love of Calvary Family Church in Kitengela is not just a church — it is our training center, our model church, and our home.

Committed to Serve in Three Ways

  1. A Church for the Community — We worship, we preach the Word, we strengthen families, and we serve our neighbors in Kitengela.
  2. A Training Center for Pastors — Pastors from across Africa come to Love of Calvary to learn ministry by watching and doing. They sit in our services, join our outreaches, and are mentored in real church life.
  3. A Model of What We Teach — We cannot train pastors to build healthy churches if we don't have one. Love of Calvary Family Church is the example of a healthy, Bible-preaching church that we train others to plant.

Our Current Need

To build a permanent building — a hall, offices, and a sanctuary that will host pastors from many nations for years to come and serve as a home for our church services.
